9. Model Limitations

  • Asymmetric sensitivity: The model is biased toward predicting REAL (100% specificity, 76% fake recall), allowing subtle deepfakes to pass as authentic.
  • Overconfidence on false negatives: Several deepfake faces were classified as REAL with > 90% confidence.
  • Upstream dataset leakage: Exactly 2 duplicate synthetic faces exist between train and test sets in the upstream repository.
  • Face detector sensitivity: Haar Cascade requires whole-head contextual margins and fails on tight 224x224 bounding boxes.

10. Recommendations

  1. Acquire an independent benchmark evaluation dataset (e.g. FaceForensics++ or Celeb-DF) with 1,000+ balanced samples.
  2. Consider upgrading the Haar Cascade face detector to an SSD or RetinaFace model for tight face crop support.
  3. Calibrate model probabilities using Platt scaling or isotonic regression to align confidence with empirical accuracy.
